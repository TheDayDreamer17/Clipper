# Design Taste & Anti-Slop Directive

## 🚫 The "AI Slop" Ban List
AI models default to the statistical average of bad web templates. The following tropes are **strictly prohibited**:
1. **The Clunky Neon Pill**: Cheap rounded pill badges with thick glowing neon borders (e.g. cyan + hot magenta cartoon rings).
2. **Template Typography**: Blindly using Impact or Arial Black slapped inside floating bounding boxes.
3. **Over-Decorated Dividers**: Multiple colored dots, laser lines, and competing gradients that create visual noise.
4. **Card / Box Enclosure Syndrome**: Trapping every piece of text inside a pill or card instead of letting typography breathe.

## 💎 Elite Gaming & Editorial Overlays (The Standard)
When designing for AAA titles like GTA VI / Vice City:
1. **Typography with Character & Restraint**:
   - Use razor-sharp, intentional letter-spacing (tracking).
   - Pair an authoritative condensed Roman numeral or display title with clean, microscopic utility labels (e.g. `8-12px uppercase, letter-spacing: 0.15em`).
2. **Authentic Materiality (Matte & Hairline)**:
   - Use 1px hairline rules with low-opacity white/titanium (`rgba(255,255,255,0.14)`) or dark carbon matte.
   - Accents must be surgical: a single 2px warm sunset or gold/coral slash, NOT rainbow borders.
3. **Content-First Hierarchy**:
   - The creator and gameplay are the heroes. The UI must feel like a premium broadcast HUD (think Formula 1, A24 film titles, or official Rockstar Games trailers), never an amateur streamer bot.
